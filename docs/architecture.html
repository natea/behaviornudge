Catch the drift before it starts

behaviornudge learns your drift pattern from screen activity your Mac already records. It finds the moments when a purposeful visit to LinkedIn, X or Instagram turned into feed-scrolling, tests which model predicts them, and runs live: a Mac notification names the task you meant to be doing, and your clicks teach it when it was wrong. Everything runs locally; only short activity keys and trimmed titles reach two cloud services.

The trap, and where the nudges fire

A drift episode starts with a purposeful visit and counts as drift when at least 3 minutes of off-task consuming follow within 15 minutes, before you return to work for at least 60 seconds.

Live engine: nudging in the moment

One Python process plus a small native helper app. Rules decide when to nudge; BehaviorGPT scores every moment in shadow mode; your clicks change what happens next.

  • Legit task: becomes your own label. Nudges stop there, and backtests relabel it on-task
